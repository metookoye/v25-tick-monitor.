3. Risk filters (research settings)

The monitor only reports “candidate conditions” when the rolling volatility is relatively low versus the loaded history, directional persistence is limited, no recent jump lockout is active, and the connection/data are healthy. These thresholds are not proven profitable.

4. Historical replay & out-of-sample check

This splits the downloaded ticks chronologically: first 70% for calibration, final 30% for evaluation. It tests whether candidate conditions are followed by relatively large price excursions over the next 10 ticks. This is a proxy-risk test, not a simulated Accumulator contract P&L or proof of an edge.
